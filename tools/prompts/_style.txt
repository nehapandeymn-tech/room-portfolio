STYLE (applies to every asset in this project): painterly digital gouache illustration with visible, chunky, confident brush strokes; forms built from flat colour shapes with soft, slightly imperfect hand-painted edges; no outlines, no photorealism, no 3D-render look; subtle canvas grain. Limited warm palette: cream, sand, honey wood, terracotta, muted teal, sage green, dusty lavender, with small saturated accents. Mood: cozy, lived-in, a little messy, personal. In the spirit of stylised animation background paintings. No readable text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no UI chrome.
The attached reference images are for mood and brushwork ONLY. Do not copy their compositions, objects or characters; paint an original scene.
